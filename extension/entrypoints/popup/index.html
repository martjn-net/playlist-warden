Playlist Warden

Fair caps, cleanup & shuffle for shared playlists

Open one of your YouTube playlists while signed in:

youtube.com/playlist?list=…

Click the “Run maintenance” button (bottom-right of the page) to capture adders and run the full chain: checks → cap → prune → shuffle. Progress shows on the button; results go to the Log.

Options: Playlists · Rules · Log · Settings (Google sign-in). Sign in there once before running the chain.
